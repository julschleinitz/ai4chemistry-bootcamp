Papers

3 resources
Kingma & Ba — "Adam: A Method for Stochastic Optimization" (2015)
Original Adam optimiser paper — still the default for most deep learning tasks.
ICLR 2015 · arXiv:1412.6980
Ioffe & Szegedy — "Batch Normalization" (2015)
Introduces batch normalization and explains its effect on training stability and learning rate sensitivity.
ICML 2015 · arXiv:1502.03167
Dropout — Srivastava et al. (2014)
Original dropout paper. Explains the co-adaptation problem and why random unit deactivation reduces overfitting.
JMLR 2014

Datasets

3 resources
MNIST
70,000 handwritten digit images — the canonical introductory benchmark for image classification and neural network training.
Yann LeCun · Free
CIFAR-10
60,000 32×32 colour images in 10 classes. Standard benchmark for CNNs and regularisation methods such as dropout and batch normalisation.
University of Toronto · Free
QM9 via PyTorch Geometric
134k small organic molecules with 19 quantum chemical properties. Ideal chemistry benchmark for testing deep learning architectures beyond standard vision tasks.
PyG · Free
