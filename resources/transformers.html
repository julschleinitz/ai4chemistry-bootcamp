Papers

4 resources
Vaswani et al. — "Attention Is All You Need" (2017)
The original transformer paper introducing scaled dot-product attention and multi-head attention.
NeurIPS 2017 · arXiv:1706.03762
Chithrananda et al. — "ChemBERTa" (2020)
Pre-trains RoBERTa on 77M SMILES from ZINC and evaluates on MoleculeNet property prediction benchmarks.
arXiv:2010.09885
Ross et al. — "Large-Scale Chemical Language Representations Capture Molecular Structure and Properties" (2022)
MolFormer: transformer pre-trained on 1.1 billion SMILES, outperforming GNNs on several benchmarks.
Nature Machine Intelligence 2022
Schwaller et al. — "Molecular Transformer: A Model for Uncertainty-Calibrated Chemical Reaction Prediction" (2019)
Applies the transformer architecture to reaction SMILES for forward prediction and retrosynthesis.
ACS Central Science 2019
