Papers

4 resources
Houlsby et al. — "Bayesian Active Learning for Classification and Preference Learning" (2011)
Introduces BALD: selecting points that maximise mutual information between model parameters and predictions.
arXiv:1112.5745
Sener & Savarese — "Active Learning for Convolutional Neural Networks: A Core-Set Approach" (2018)
Core-set selection as a geometry-based AL strategy: select points that minimise the largest uncovered distance.
ICLR 2018 · arXiv:1708.00489
Reker & Schneider — "Active-learning strategies in computer-assisted drug discovery" (2015)
Chemistry-specific review of active learning applications in QSAR and virtual screening.
Drug Discovery Today 2015
Lookman et al. — "Active learning in materials science with emphasis on adaptive sampling in experimental design" (2019)
Review of AL applied to accelerate materials discovery via adaptive experimental design.
npj Computational Materials 2019

Datasets

3 resources
QM9
134k molecules with 19 DFT properties. Its large size and known ground truth make it well-suited for simulating pool-based active learning loops.
PyG · Free
ANI-1 Dataset
20M DFT energies for small organic molecules across conformational space. Built via active learning — a real example of AL-driven dataset construction.
Isayev group · Free
MoleculeNet
Curated collection of molecular property datasets (ESOL, BBBP, Tox21, BACE) with standardised splits. Practical for AL experiments on real drug-like tasks.
