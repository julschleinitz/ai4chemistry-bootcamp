Papers

4 resources
Yao et al. — "ReAct: Synergizing Reasoning and Acting in Language Models" (2023)
Introduces the ReAct framework interleaving reasoning traces and action calls. The foundation for tool-using LLM agents.
ICLR 2023 · arXiv:2210.03629
Lewis et al. — "Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks" (2020)
Foundational RAG paper combining a retrieval module with a seq2seq generator.
NeurIPS 2020 · arXiv:2005.11401
Boiko et al. — "Autonomous chemical research with large language models" (2023)
Coscientist: LLM-orchestrated autonomous chemical synthesis — web search, code execution, and robotic control.
Nature 2023
Bran et al. — "ChemCrow: Augmenting large-language models with chemistry tools" (2024)
Chemistry agent with 18 expert tools — demonstrates tool use improving accuracy and reducing hallucination.
Nature Machine Intelligence 2024
