Papers

4 resources
Kingma & Welling — "Auto-Encoding Variational Bayes" (2014)
Original VAE paper. Essential reference for the ELBO objective and reparameterisation trick.
ICLR 2014 · arXiv:1312.6114
Ho et al. — "Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Models" (2020)
Foundational DDPM paper connecting denoising score matching to a variational bound.
NeurIPS 2020 · arXiv:2006.11239
Hoogeboom et al. — "Equivariant Diffusion for Molecule Generation in 3D" (2022)
EDM: diffusion in continuous atom-coordinate space using SE(3)-equivariant networks for valid 3-D molecule generation.
ICML 2022 · arXiv:2203.17003
Gómez-Bombarelli et al. — "Automatic Chemical Design Using a Data-Driven Continuous Representation of Molecules" (2018)
Proposes the molecular VAE latent space for Bayesian optimisation of drug-like properties. Highly cited.
ACS Central Science 2018
